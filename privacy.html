Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • How Innovexa Studio collects, uses, and protects your data.

Innovexa Studio respects your privacy and is committed to safeguarding the personal and project data you share when using our website, client portal, and support chat.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication identifiers provided when you sign up or log in via Firebase Authentication.
  • Order & Contact Details: Phone/WhatsApp numbers, project notes, reference video URLs, selected services, and invoice records submitted during checkout.
  • Support & Community Submissions: Messages sent in our Live Support Chat, client reviews, and uploaded showcase images.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process orders, generate printable invoices, and send automated order confirmations via EmailJS.
  • To communicate directly with you regarding project progress, deployment setup, and customer support tickets.
  • To validate promotional coupons, manage service availability, and prevent fraudulent checkout activity.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Storage

We rely on trusted industry infrastructure to store and process data securely:

  • Google Firebase (Auth & Firestore): Used for encrypted account login, real-time order tracking, and support chat storage.
  • Cloudinary CDN: Used for hosting uploaded review photos and showcase media.
  • EmailJS: Used exclusively to dispatch transactional invoice and order confirmation emails.

4. Data Sharing & Confidentiality

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information, email address, or phone number to third parties. Project credentials (such as bot tokens, hosting logins, or API keys shared during setup) are treated as strictly confidential.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may view your order history and profile metadata anytime from your Client Profile. To request modification or permanent deletion of your account data, contact us through our Support Page or at muntasirtonmoy10@gmail.com.
