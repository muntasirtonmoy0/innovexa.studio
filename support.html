Quick answers about ordering, payments, delivery timelines, and custom development.

Select any package from our service pages and click Order Direct (or add multiple services to your Cart). On the checkout page, complete your transfer via bKash (bKash-to-bKash or Card/Bank-to-bKash) or UCB Bank Account, enter your Transaction ID (TrxID), and submit. Your invoice is generated immediately and emailed to you.
